How to use it

  1. Click where you want to type, in any app: Word, WhatsApp, email, a form.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+D or click the round mic button floating on your screen.
  3. Speak. Your words appear each time you pause.
  4. Press the shortcut again or Done. SayIt types it where your cursor is. Esc cancels.

Drag the round button anywhere. Right-click it to hide it. SayIt also lives in your system tray.

Groq key (free, recommended)

For speech you need one of: a Groq key (free and fastest), an OpenAI key, or a Google Gemini key (add those under "Other AI" below). Claude can fix grammar but can't listen to audio, so a Claude key alone isn't enough. Get a free key → Sign in with Google, click "Create API key", paste it here.

What should happen to your words?

Speech

Separate with commas. They'll be spelled exactly like this.

Click the box, then press the keys you want (e.g. Ctrl+Shift+Space).

Appearance

Drag the top of the box to move it, and the bottom-right corner to make it bigger or smaller. SayIt remembers the size.

Other AI for grammar (optional)

Choose who fixes the grammar, and paste that provider's key. Each provider's key is kept separately, so you can switch back and forth. Groq is usually the fastest (about 0.5 s); Claude is about 0.8 s. A Gemini or OpenAI key here can also be used for speech if you don't have a Groq key.
