How to use it

  1. Click inside any text box on a website.
  2. Tap the SayIt icon in your toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+S.
  3. Speak. SayIt fixes the grammar while you're still talking.
  4. Press Done or the shortcut again. Your text drops into the box. Esc cancels.

The first time on each website, Chrome asks for the microphone. Click Allow.
